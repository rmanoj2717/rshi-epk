Bio

RSHÏ is an Atlanta DJ whose job is simple: throw a proper party. Raised in music from a young age, he plays like a performer, not a background playlist, shifting through tech, indie, afro, and deep house to keep the floor on its toes. Drawing from the dancefloor focus of artists like Chris Stussy and Franky Rizardo, his sets are about selection and room control: raise the energy, hold it, and get everyone moving.

Performed at venues such as Jagger Suite, Glam104, and Embr Lounge.
